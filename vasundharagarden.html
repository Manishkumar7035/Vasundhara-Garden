A LITTLE ABOUT US

Hospitality with heart.

Vasundhara Garden is a welcoming family restaurant and banquet hall designed to make every occasion special. We offer a comfortable and pleasant environment where families and friends can enjoy delicious food, spend quality time together, and celebrate memorable moments. Our spacious banquet hall is suitable for weddings, birthday parties, anniversaries, family functions, meetings, and other special events. With tasty food, a beautiful atmosphere, spacious seating, and friendly service, Vasundhara Garden is a perfect place for both dining and celebrations. We look forward to making your visit enjoyable and your special occasions unforgettable.
